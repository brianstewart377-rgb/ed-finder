No database, service, production system, credential, private user state, or V2 archive was accessed. “Implemented” below means implemented in source or a checked-in machine-readable contract, not populated in production. Population and coverage are therefore `UNKNOWN_COVERAGE` unless a repository artifact itself proves otherwise.

Truth vocabulary used here:

- **AUTHORITATIVE** — source-owned factual observation or identity, subject to its stated freshness and provenance.
- **DERIVED** — calculation, inference, prediction, aggregation, or reviewed interpretation.
- **PLANNED** — user or planner proposal; never built fact.
- **SCHEMATIC** — unresolved, approximate, or display-only placement/association.
- **AMBIENT** — decorative rendering with no mechanics meaning.
- **UNAVAILABLE** — no current contract or implementation was found.
- **UNKNOWN_COVERAGE** — shape exists, but real population was not measured.

## Ownership and dependency finding

The three repositories agree on the high-level boundary but are not runtime-integrated:

`CRE reviewed research -> versioned KnowledgeProjection/publication -> CPE assessment and plan construction -> ED-Finder orchestration/presentation -> renderer-neutral SpatialContribution`

- CRE owns research evidence, provenance, observations, claims, mechanics, contradictions, confidence, planner-safe knowledge releases, and observed-state publications (`CRE README.md`; `ontology/entities.md`; `planner/knowledge_projection_rules.md`). It explicitly says it is not a database implementation or production application.
- CPE owns programme-specific assessment/comparison and player-specific plan construction, sequencing, alternatives, and plan outputs (`CPE README.md`). Its current state is documentation-only: no runtime, API, database, shared package, storage model, or implementation structure.
- ED-Finder owns the runnable product, current data/API surfaces, exploration, presentation, and its existing Colony Cockpit. CRE is not wired into ED-Finder at runtime (`CLAUDE.md`, “Three-repo architecture”). ED-Finder must not manufacture missing CRE or CPE truth.
- Stage 27 consumes those owners through adapters. Babylon, or any future renderer, must not import CRE mechanics or CPE planning logic and must not infer lifecycle, validity, confidence, or completion from visual properties.

## CRE ontology audit conclusions

### What CRE actually defines

CRE's ontology is a conceptual graph, not an object API. `ontology/relationships.md` gives the relevant graph explicitly: System has Bodies and Facilities; Facility is located on Body, occupies Slot, uses StationType, exposes Economy, plays Role, buys/sells Commodity, and links to another Facility. Evidence supports or contradicts Observations and Claims; Mechanics are supported/contradicted/tested; KnowledgeProjections derive from reviewed layers; PlannerRecommendations consume projections.

The important integration rules are concrete:

1. `System` should use `system_id64` when available; display name is insufficient (`ontology/entities.md`, System).
2. Body identity is stable and system-linked; body display name alone is insufficient (`ontology/entities.md`, Body).
3. CRE's root installation object is `Facility`. “Station”, “Port”, “Settlement”, and “Installation” are subtypes/labels, while `Station Type` is a classifier. There is no current exported `FacilityInstance` symbol.
4. Facility identity needs system, body when known, facility type, and external identifier when available. A same-name match cannot resolve identity (`ontology/identity_and_provenance_rules.md`, IP-0001/IP-0002).
5. Slot capacity, occupancy, and facility identity are separate. Predicted/open capacity is not built infrastructure (IP-0010 and `ontology/relationships.md`).
6. Research evidence, operational data, and user plans remain distinct identity layers (IP-0012). Direct observation, parsed claim, inferred mechanic, and published projection must not collapse (IP-0005).
7. Confidence is contextual, versioned, and layer-specific. Contradictions and negative evidence lower confidence; patch drift decays it (`ontology/confidence_model_register.md`).

### CRE schema readiness

`schemas/colony_state.schema.json` is the closest machine-readable Digital Twin precursor. It models a patch-aware snapshot with bodies, facilities, market links, economy snapshots, commodity observations, constraints, confidence and source references. Its companion document explicitly says it is documentation-first and not a production database schema. `architecture/cre_digital_twin.md` deliberately does not create a new schema and labels its JSON example illustrative only.

Therefore Stage 27 may reserve adapter shapes and truth classes, but must not call the colony-state schema a published CRE runtime contract. A CRE release/API decision is required before ED-Finder consumes it as authoritative live data.

### CRE conflicts and unresolved contracts to preserve

- **Lifecycle vocabulary drift:** ontology, colony-state schema, and Digital Twin design use overlapping but different terms. Reconciliation belongs to CRE before typed interchange.
- **Identity gap:** `facility_ref` and `body_ref` are strings in the draft schema without a normative encoding. Stage 27's conceptual `BodyRef { systemId64, bodyId }` should not be claimed as already adopted by CRE.
- **Location gap:** U-0011 explicitly leaves public-evidence body linkage unresolved. Spatial adapters must support `system_only`/unresolved attachments.
- **Mechanics uncertainty:** weak links, routing aggregation, body-modifier stacking, dependency strictness, commodity derivation, pad metadata and demolition effects remain registered unknowns/contradictions. Spatial presentation cannot resolve them visually.
- **Dangling source references:** CRE IP-0012/IP-0013 and confidence CM-0001A/CM-0006A cite `architecture/runtime_boundary_design.md`, `architecture/knowledge_projection_design.md`, and/or `architecture/planner_execution_architecture.md`, none of which exists at audited commit `e6d34cf`. The rules are present in canonical registers, but their cited supporting paths are incomplete and must be repaired by CRE owners.
- **Digital Twin phrasing:** `architecture/cre_digital_twin.md` contrasts the Twin with an “existing system map,” while CRE's top-level README says it is not a production app and the audited repository has no map code. Treat this as product-boundary prose, not evidence of a CRE renderer.

## Stage 27 acceptance implications

- System and facility adapters must accept unresolved attachments without guessing, and all body attachments must be system-scoped.
- A contribution carries representation class, source/provenance reference, owner, freshness/patch context where relevant, and confidence only in the owner's semantics.
- Built, observed, inferred, preview, planned, projected, schematic, historical and ambient states must remain distinguishable even if an owner later normalises the lifecycle vocabulary.
- Digital Twin information layers onto `SystemScene`; it does not create a second renderer or competing map.
- CRE evidence details, contradictions and rationale remain accessible DOM/UI concerns; the spatial runtime may identify and highlight targets but cannot become the evidence store.
- No Stage 27 renderer derives mechanics, slot counts, commodity coverage, facility validity, plan fit, lifecycle, body association, or confidence from coordinates, icon choice, color, opacity, or proximity.
- Unknown coverage, missing hierarchy, uncertain association and absent sibling APIs are valid states and must have deterministic fixtures.

## Genuine owner decisions before later integration

1. **CRE:** publish or reject the draft colony-state JSON Schema as an interchange basis; define normative `system_ref`, `body_ref`, and `facility_ref` encodings and version compatibility.
2. **CRE:** reconcile facility lifecycle vocabularies and define how current, historical, observed, inferred and projected snapshots are represented together.
3. **CRE:** decide the reviewed KnowledgeProjection/release transport (versioned files/package/API) and publication threshold; repair dangling architecture references.
4. **CPE:** define its first plan/assessment contracts, stable plan and option identities, persistence ownership migration (if any), and explicit command semantics.
5. **Cross-repo owners:** define a versioned adapter compatibility policy. Until then, ED-Finder may reserve types but must treat CRE/CPE runtime contributions as unavailable.
