# Archive and Historical Evidence

Archived and historical material preserves decisions, receipts, experiments,
closeouts, and provenance. Its presence never authorizes current implementation,
deployment, database work, recovery, or other operations.

For current direction, start at [`../../README.md`](../../README.md) and follow
the authority links there. [`../ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) controls programme
status, sequencing, and authorization;
[`../operations/infrastructure-status.md`](../operations/infrastructure-status.md)
controls the production and recovery boundary. Current product, architecture,
stack, and browser-validation authorities override conflicting historical text
on their respective surfaces.

Old “current”, “next”, “approved”, “production”, or stage-authorization wording
must be read in its original historical context. It does not become current
because a file remains in the repository or because an old plan was once
completed. This includes V2/Hetzner receipts, Stage 17–26 plans and closeouts,
Stage 25/26 map authority, the historical R3F/Three result, and superpowers map
plans.

History should normally be preserved rather than rewritten to match the present:
it is evidence of what happened and why. Add or improve a front-door/index note
when clarification is needed. Do not mass-move historical documents as part of
small authority cleanups; moves require their own reviewed, link-aware scope.
This packet moves no historical documents.
