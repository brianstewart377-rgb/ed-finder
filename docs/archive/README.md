# Historical and Archived Documentation

Files in this directory preserve rationale, audits, receipts, incident context,
and implementation evidence. They are non-authoritative and must not be used to
authorize current product work, architecture, deployment, runtime, database,
recovery, or rollback actions.

The same rule applies to older stage documents that have not yet been physically
moved here. Their current location does not give them current authority. This
fast authority cleanup deliberately leaves physical retirement to a separate
archive pass.

For current work, begin at [`../../README.md`](../../README.md) and follow the
small authority chain there. [`../ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) always wins on
programme order and decision gates; the named product, architecture, stack,
browser, and infrastructure authorities win on their respective surfaces.

Historical documents may be cited to explain why a decision was made or to
preserve test/migration evidence. If they disagree with current authority, keep
the disagreement as history and follow current authority. Never reconstruct a
V2 production procedure from an archived document.
