# ED-Finder vs Colonization Mega Guide v2.3.0 (2.4.0 WIP) — mechanics audit

Read-only audit, 2026-10-21. The guide is the supplied PDF (121 pages). Page numbers below are **PDF page numbers**, matching each page's own "Page N" footer. The guide's table of contents is off by one in places (it lists "Enabling Services" as 44, but the section is on PDF page 45). The repo is a local working copy of ed-finder `main` (no commit was recorded for it; the same-day CRE/CPE audit used `c396eb21`). The guide's extracted text is committed at `docs/reference/colonisation/sources/mega-guide-v2.3.0/`. Nothing in the repo was changed.

## Executive summary

1. **36 findings:** 11 FROZEN (9 Ratings V4.0, 2 Finder archetype), 14 PLANNER/SIMULATOR, 4 COPY, 7 DOC. Four of the FROZEN findings are deliberate and documented. Separately there are 13 gaps and 1 game change made after the guide.
2. **Simulator (#1).** Colony-type ports do not inherit body economies. Only the planner's placeholder `colony_ship` does. A real Coriolis orbiting an Earth-like world (ELW) comes out as `{'Colony': 2.0}`; the guide (p71) gives Tourism 1.4, HighTech 1.4, Agriculture 1.4, Military 1.0. Confirmed by running the code.
3. **Simulator (#2).** Construction-point (CP) costs are wrong by an order of magnitude, and tiers are mislabelled. The escalation table is 16/20/26… yellow for T2 and 40/52/… yellow plus 16/24/… green for T3; the guide (p27) says T2 costs 3/3/5/7/9 yellow and T3 costs 6/6/12/18/24 green, from one shared counter. The importer also tags every outpost and the T3 planetary port as tier 2. So a large T2 settlement is charged 16 yellow, and a Coriolis after two outposts goes to −57 yellow. Confirmed by running the code.
4. **Simulator (#3).** The service model has no port-tier defaults. A Coriolis or Orbis shows only a commodity market. The shipyard rule is actually the guide's outfitting rule, and space outposts are not blocked from shipyards (guide p45–46, p63: "cannot have shipyards, period").
5. **Ratings V4 (#4).** Combined native + modifier inheritance scores 85 against 75 for native alone. The guide says the two "do NOT stack" ("100%, NOT 200%", p50). Ringed or geological HMCs and geological icy or rocky-ice bodies are inflated in the live Finder. A fix needs a V4.1 ruleset after go-live.
6. **Ratings V4 (#5).** The terraformable Agriculture boost (+10) is applied in full, but the guide marks it "PRESENTLY BUGGED – NOT WORKING" (p55, p93). The repo's own `mechanics-confidence.md` calls it speculative. The "later fixes" claimed in the evidence doc have no source cited. Fix in V4.1.
7. **Simulator body table and link modifiers** (`colonisation_rules.py`, `link_rules.py`): stars get no Military, Ammonia HighTech is treated as a modifier, and most ring/geological/biological modifiers are missing. Link modifiers are multiplicative custom deltas instead of the guide's flat ±0.4, and geologicals (rather than volcanism) boost Extraction.
8. **Exposure.** The simulator, optimiser and planner are mounted API routes (`main.py:396-400`), but `apps/web` (V3) has no planner UI. The PLANNER findings reach players only through the API or the legacy frontend. The FROZEN findings reach players through the Finder.
9. **Not modelled at all:** system score / credits per week, population (the guide now gives exact formulae), system stats, demolition / exclusivity, and the Service / Terraforming / Contraband economies.
10. **The guide itself may be stale.** It excludes the 4.2.2.0 (11 Nov 2025) facility-stat nerf. 2.4.0 is still WIP. Several of its items are marked bug, "To verify" or TBD, and it contradicts itself in places (listed in the post-guide section). The 4.2.2.0 patch also changed where EPCs are produced.

## Gaps — in the guide, not modelled by the app (brief)

1. **System score and credits per week** (p81, Appendix C p99): Score × ~10,000 × (1 + Happiness). Per-facility scores: T3 starports 15, Coriolis/Asteroid 8, hubs 5, T1 planetary outposts 4, and so on. Population is excluded.
2. **Population model** (p76-79): linear for 14 ticks, then logistic, P = K/(1+e^(−0.0168(x−100))). Ground inflection is at 120 days. K depends on body and tier. Production scales with √population. Only the first-built highest-tier port gets the body boost (p91, "To verify"). Demolition may not reduce population (p80, bug?).
3. **System stats** (p61-64): security, wealth, standard of living, tech level (TL35 gate, granted automatically by a T2/T3 port), development level 0-20, happiness. 4.2.2.0 changed these (see below).
4. **Rules and limits** (p17-24, p31, p43): 25 m claim cost, 15 LY claim range, 24 h to deploy the beacon, 4 weeks to build the primary port, exclusivity window (30 min architect, 23.5 h squadron), up to 5 concurrent constructions, primary must be a spaceport at a fixed slot (cancel only, never demolish), demolition at the weekly tick with dependency order, slot/CP refunds and negative CP.
5. **Economies:** Service, Terraforming and Contraband (p49, p72). Orbital-only vs surface-only goods: Insulating Membranes orbital; CMM/Ceramic Composites surface (p33, p91).
6. **Economy pair compatibility matrix and Appendix D overlaps** (p72, p100-114); same-strength alphabetical tie-break (p71, "To be confirmed"); the "flipping" strategy (p95).
7. **Government effects:** black-market ethos (p47); Anarchy/Dictatorship for Combat Stabilisers and Battle Weapons; Anarchy full shipyard (p20, p63).
8. **Faction seeding** on a claim: 60/20/13/6 (p20).
9. **Agriculture planetary-port bug** (p88, p93): prefer orbital farms.
10. **Station interiors** by top economy (p36-42).
11. **Architect 3% discount** after the 10th installation (p12).
12. **Converted-port shielding** from incoming weak links, and "nesting" strategies (p60, p96-97). Only partly modelled in the topology.
13. **Specialised-port baselines** of 0.5 / 1.0 (p48). Not in V4, which models natural potential only.

## Changes in the game after the guide

- **The guide's own caveats:** its header says the 4.2.2.0 "‑70% first, now ‑25% nerf" is not incorporated, and the 2.4.0 WIP row lists "TO ADD: Dodec details; Impact of nerf". Items it marks as bug, "To verify" or TBD: the terraformable Agriculture modifier (bug), planetary Agriculture ports (bug), VG/Pioneer requirements not applied (bug), demolition not reducing population, port-to-port pass-through (probable bug), the population table (mostly TBD), same-strength ordering, a missing 2nd-faction case, and the beacon re-claim lockout.
- **Places where the guide contradicts itself** (treat these sections with care):
  - p24 cites "patch notes from 4.4.1.2"; this should be 4.2.1.2.
  - p28 example "T3, T3, T2, T2 → 16 Yellow" does not match its own table (5 + 7 = 12).
  - p72 rates Ind–Ext red, but p95 calls Ind+Ext compatible.
  - p71 suggests alphabetical tie-breaking, but its own ELW example lists Tourism/HighTech ahead of Agriculture at equal 1.4.
- **4.2.2.0 "Dodec Update" (11 Nov 2025):** "the initial starport in a system now having a greater impact on development, security, and tech levels, while subsequent facilities have reduced effects … This change is retroactive"; "EPCs will now be produced and sold at surface port industrial economies". This is the nerf the guide excludes. It overrides any guide reasoning about facility stat chevrons and the EPC FAQ (p91). ## Method and limits

- **Guide:** read in full from the layout extract (lines 1–4419) and mapped to PDF pages using form feeds. Image-only or garbled pages were rendered and viewed: services pp45-46, compatibility matrix p72, population formula p78, codex p115-116. Codex pages 117-121 were not transcribed; they reproduce the in-game codex.
- **App:** every claim comes from reading the code. P1, P3, P4, P5 and P9 were also confirmed by running the real modules against the bundled catalogue in an isolated scratch directory, using `python3 -I` and a symlinked package. That run used CPython 3.13, not the repo's 3.14 lane; the code paths involved are plain Python.
- **Not verified:**
  - which `facility_templates` catalogue production loads (DB seed or JSON);
  - whether the evidence doc's "2026 fixes" for terraformable or tidal lock exist;
  - how far the legacy `frontend/` and `packages/planner-core` (journal import only in `apps/web`) still reach players;
  - the frozen V4 freeze/cohort numbers (not re-run);
  - the official 4.2.2.0 patch text.
- **No older guide copy exists to compare against.** The audit compares the guide with what the app encodes, not one guide version with another.
