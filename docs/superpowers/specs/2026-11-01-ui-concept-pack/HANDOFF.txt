FINDER DETAILS
Any plus Paradise World, Mining Hub, Manufacturing Hub, Megacomplex, Research Hub, Stronghold, Population Capital, Flexible Multi-Role Colony.
18 count sliders: Planets (9) - Earth-like, water worlds, ammonia worlds, terraformable, gas giants, high metal content, metal-rich, rocky, icy.
Stars (4) - black holes, neutron stars, white dwarfs, other stars.
Features (5) - landable, walkable, rings, biological signals, geological signals.
Count filters are distinct from ranking weights. V3 weight dimensions were not defined in the reviewed source; do not infer them from old code or these concepts.
Any mode: Best Colony Potential. Selected mode: fit for the selected archetype. Preserve API-supported modes and filters.
